# Example Inputs And Outputs

## Example 1

### Input

Use a classic-book case to explain why users stay trapped inside large platforms.

### Expected Shape

- likely book match: `A Brief History of Time` or `Out of Control`
- source case: black hole or self-reinforcing systems
- principle: exit cost and system gravity
- writing angle: platforms do not only attract users, they raise the price of leaving

## Example 2

### Input

I want to explain personal branding without sounding fake.

### Expected Shape

- likely book match: `Sapiens`
- source case: shared myths and coordination
- principle: a brand works when many people can hold the same compressed story about you
- writing angle: personal brand is not self-praise, it is a socially portable story

## Example 3

### Input

Explain procrastination in a way normal people can actually use.

### Expected Shape

- likely book match: `Atomic Habits` with `Thinking, Fast and Slow`
- source case: habit loops and friction
- principle: behavior follows cue, ease, and reward more than identity claims alone
- writing angle: procrastination is often bad environment design, not pure laziness
